Research Interests

Anomaly detection

Multimodal sensing, computer vision, and explainable AI to detect anomalous motion patterns and predict surface quality in precision manufacturing.

Computer visionProcess monitoring

Synthetic data generation

Generative models that produce realistic, physics-consistent process data when real manufacturing observations are scarce.

Generative AIPhysics-conditioned diffusion

Uncertainty quantification

Tracing uncertainty from visual detection through tracking to physical measurements for more reliable manufacturing decisions.

Trustworthy AIMotion analysis

Manufacturing cybersecurity

Reinforcement learning and controller-level modeling to understand cyberattacks and strengthen the resilience of manufacturing systems.

Reinforcement learningCyber-physical systems
